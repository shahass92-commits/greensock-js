07 / CORRIDOR PROCESS

From booking
to verified invoice.

  1. STEP 1

    Book & dispatch

    Roster and bookings become trips with a unique Trip ID, an assigned vehicle and a verified driver.

  2. STEP 2

    Run & verify

    GPS route, actual KM, pickup and drop are checked against the booking, then the customer verifies and the trip is locked.

  3. STEP 3

    Reconcile & invoice

    AI flags exceptions for review. A person approves, and the invoice links back to every trip it bills.
